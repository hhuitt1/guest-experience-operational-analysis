Guest Experience & Operational Performance Analysis

Business Problem

Explain:

• guest satisfaction
• wait times
• downtime
• complaint trends
• staffing insights

Tools Used
• SQL
• SQLite
• Tableau Public
• Excel

Key Insights: 
• Animal Kingdom and Hollywood Studios reported the highest average guest satisfaction scores across all parks.
• Average attraction wait times remained relatively consistent resort-wide, indicating strong overall guest demand across parks.
• Staff Interaction and Cleanliness represented the highest guest complaint categories, highlighting opportunities to improve frontline 
service consistency and operational maintenance standards.
• Slinky Dog Dash, Kilimanjaro Safaris, and Frozen Ever After experienced the highest attraction downtime totals, suggesting potential 
reliability and throughput challenges for high-demand attractions.
• Higher attraction wait times did not consistently reduce guest satisfaction, indicating that strong attraction demand and perceived 
experience value may offset queue frustration.
